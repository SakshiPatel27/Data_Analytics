# Olympic Games Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
This Tableau dashboard provides comprehensive analysis of Olympic Games data spanning over a century (1900-2020). The analysis covers medal distributions, athlete demographics, sport participation, geographic trends, and performance analytics across multiple Olympic Games.

### 5. **Temporal Analysis**

**District Count of NOCs Over Time:**

- **Mid-Century Growth (1950-1980)**: Significant expansion (~100+ NOCs)
- **Modern Era (1980-2020)**: Plateau around 200+ NOCs
- Notable disruptions: 1916, 1940, 1944 (World War I & II cancellations)

**District Count of Athletes Over Time:**
- **1900-1920**: <10K athletes
- **1920-1970**: Steady growth to 4K+ athletes

- Recent Olympics show increased participation

## Analysis Insights

### Historical Evolution
1. **Participation Growth**: Olympic Games have expanded dramatically since 1900
2. **Inclusivity**: Increased participation of women and diverse nations
3. **Professionalization**: Modern athletes show higher specialization
4. **Globalization**: Representation from all major continents

### Geographic Dynamics
- **Developed Nations**: Historically dominated medal counts
- **Rising Powers**: China and other emerging economies gaining prominence
- **Regional Variations**: Different sports popular in different regions
- **Infrastructure Impact**: Hosting Nations show boost in athletes

### Athletic Performance
1. **Age Optimization**: Peak athletic performance ages 25-35
2. **Gender Evolution**: Increasing female participation
3. **Physical Specialization**: Sport-specific body requirements evident
4. **Longevity**: Some sports accommodate athletes across wider age ranges

### Sport Development
- **Traditional Sports**: Athletics, Swimming, Gymnastics maintain dominance
- **Emerging Sports**: New disciplines continuously added
- **Physical Demands**: Clear correlation between sport type and athlete physiology
- **Event Expansion**: Recent Olympics feature 66+ sports
